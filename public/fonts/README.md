# Self-hosted Arabic fonts

- **Amiri** (weights 400 and 700) and **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (weights 300–700) are the Arabic subsets previously emitted by `next/font/google` for this application.
- They are self-hosted to remove the runtime/build dependency on Google Fonts. Both font families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; retain this attribution and the upstream licence when redistributing or replacing the files.
- Upstream sources: [Amiri](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Amiri) and [IBM Plex Sans Arabic](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans+Arabic).
