# Competitor benchmark and design acceptance — 2026-08-27

## Scope

The public competitor was inspected only to identify journey and trust gaps. Its design, wording, assets, and claims are not implementation sources.

## Observed opportunities

- Reduce ambiguity by routing a visitor from need → suitable format → real published detail → account/checkout.
- Keep books, learning, sessions, and workshops visible as distinct paths rather than a dense undifferentiated catalog.
- Treat testimonials, press, prices, availability, and sold-out status as governed facts; show nothing as real without a database source and owner approval.
- Keep the current Arabic RTL identity and original woman-in-niqab expression while improving information hierarchy and operational control.

## Acceptance rules

- Public content is either published database content or an explicitly safe editorial fallback that cannot be mistaken for a transaction or third-party claim.
- Every new Admin mutation is permission-checked, validated, persisted, audited, and consumed by a public/customer experience.
- Legal drafts and private previews are non-indexable.
- Mobile acceptance includes 390px layout, touch targets, no horizontal overflow, and parity with desktop actions.

## Accepted homepage direction

The homepage now has a fixed, typed section vocabulary: hero, trust, pathways, guided start, editorial feature, published offer, articles, verified testimonials, and final call-to-action. Admin may reorder, show/hide, and edit structured fields without arbitrary HTML or component injection. Publishing fails closed if hero, pathways, or final CTA is absent.
