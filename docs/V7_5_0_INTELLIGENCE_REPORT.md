# V7.5.0 Business Intelligence Report

## Goal

The owner should understand the business in one minute: revenue, products, learning, bookings, offers, and customer health.

## Required report families

- Revenue by product and month.
- Course enrollment and completion.
- Book access and downloads.
- Workshop registration and attendance.
- Booking conversion and cancellations.
- Coupon and offer performance.
- Customer activity and inactive learners.

## V8 direction

Reports must never rely on fake production numbers. Until Supabase is populated, admin report routes show structural readiness and connect to real tables/views when deployed.
