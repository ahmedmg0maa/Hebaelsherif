## C. Manual payment launch path — approved technical model, missing business details

The approved first payment method is manual administrative review. The implemented model is:

- customer selects only a configured manual method (InstaPay, wallet, or bank transfer), submits a proof, and enters an auditable pending-review state;
- no entitlement is granted before a protected administrative approval;
- approve/reject, refunds, role changes, and operational payment-setting changes require fresh AAL2 assurance and produce audit records;
- order/payment transition checks prevent invalid or duplicate approval; and
- electronic gateway functionality remains disabled/post-launch unless selected later.

The owner must enter the real, publishable receiving method through Admin — account-holder name plus one of the exact configured manual methods — and approve the displayed proof instructions. Do not place bank details or private account credentials in this document or in chat.
