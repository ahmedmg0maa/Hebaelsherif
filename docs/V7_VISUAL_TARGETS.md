# V7 Visual Targets

Current delivered version: V7.0.1

This document converts the supplied mockups into implementation targets. It is intentionally versioned by patch so the platform does not claim V8 maturity before the intervening gates pass.

## V7.0.1 Premium Homepage

Target: luxury Arabic RTL homepage with a calm feminine editorial rhythm.

Required structure:

- Top utility/header area with premium wordmark.
- Navigation with active state.
- Login/register or account actions.
- Hero with emotional headline and two-color emphasis.
- Portrait/media area.
- Primary deep-teal CTA and secondary taupe/gold outline CTA.
- Trust/value strip.
- Category cards for books, courses/programs, sessions, and workshops.
- Limited offer countdown.
- Featured articles, testimonials, newsletter, and footer.

Color behavior:

- Ivory and soft white dominate the screen.
- Deep teal anchors CTA and structure.
- Burgundy appears only as emotional emphasis, offer/progress emphasis, or alert.
- Antique gold is used for icons, thin dividers, and premium details.
- Cobalt is a small highlight only.

## V7.0.2 Product Discovery

Target: premium educational ecommerce page, more mature than a generic card grid.

Required structure:

- Hero for courses/products.
- Category strip with icons and counts.
- Search/filter controls.
- Featured course/product cards with image, title, level/category, duration, rating, price, offer badge, and CTA.
- Limited-time offer module.
- Comparison panel: "With Heba ElSherif" versus traditional learning.
- Benefits row, testimonials, bottom CTA.

## V7.1.0 Learner Dashboard And Course Player

Target: real LMS feeling, useful and calm.

Required layout:

- Right dark-teal sidebar.
- Top user bar.
- Learner dashboard heading.
- Progress summary, streak, upcoming session.
- Large course player.
- Curriculum/lesson panel.
- Resources, downloads, book progress, notes, achievements.

Course player:

- Large video area with play button and timeline.
- Lesson/module titles.
- Previous/next lesson controls.
- Mark complete.
- Resources and personal notes.
- Curriculum sidebar with completed, current, and locked states.
- Visible progress percentage and continue learning flow.

## Admin OS Target

Target: enterprise Arabic RTL admin, dense but elegant.

Required shell:

- Right sidebar in deep teal.
- Top bar with search, notifications, and profile.
- Overview KPI cards.
- Revenue chart and booking donut.
- Payment approvals queue.
- Recent bookings table.
- Today schedule, recent customers, activity log, quick summary strip.

Required sections:

- Overview, products, books, courses, curriculum, workshops.
- Bookings, orders, payments, users, coupons, offers.
- Pages, articles, media, reviews, reports, settings.
- Roles, audit logs, security, system.

Required interactions:

- Search, filters, pagination, status badges.
- CSV export where practical.
- Permission-aware actions and server-side checks.
- Audit timeline for important actions.

## V8 Flagship Platform

Target: complete premium ecosystem, not just a website.

Required:

- Strong flagship hero.
- Product ecosystem for books, courses, workshops, and 1:1 sessions.
- Journey steps.
- Featured program.
- Offer/countdown.
- Community/testimonials.
- Newsletter/footer.
- Clear customer path.

V8 can only be claimed when public site, LMS, books, commerce, bookings, workshops, admin, offers/coupons, reports, security, CMS, performance, and deployment checks are all complete.
