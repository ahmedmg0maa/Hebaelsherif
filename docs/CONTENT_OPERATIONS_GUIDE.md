# Content operations guide

## Publishing principles

- Publish only owner-approved titles, copy, prices, images, rights metadata, policies, and media.
- Empty state is preferable to invented offers, testimonials, schedules, qualifications, availability, or outcomes.
- Add useful alt text and source/rights information for each public image. Select media from the library; do not paste an unverified remote path.

## Daily owner workflow

1. Open **System status** and resolve every blocked readiness item before making a public promise.
2. Create or edit products, courses, books, workshops, articles, and services in their specialized screens.
3. Keep drafts unpublished until title, slug, description, cover/alt, price or free status, and relevant policy are reviewed.
4. For a service, configure Cairo availability, exceptions/blackouts, manual slots, and the per-service booking policy before publishing it.
5. Use preview and revision history before publish/schedule. Confirm the public page at desktop and 390px after material design changes.
6. Review pending payments, booking changes, customer notes, and moderation queues from the operations screens; each action must produce a clear result and audit record.

## Legal and communication limits

Terms, privacy, refund, contact response times, reminders, and email campaigns remain draft/blocked until the owner and relevant legal/provider review complete. Do not rewrite draft copy as a published commitment merely to populate a page.

## Incident handling

If content, availability, payment, or delivery behaves unexpectedly, unpublish/disable the affected public surface where safe, preserve the audit trail, and record the issue in `KNOWN_ISSUES.md`. Do not delete booking/order/delivery records to hide an error.
